Eight quick calls. Is it something you report, or not? Answer, read why, and move on to the next one.

Nothing you answer is recorded, and you can play as often as you like. All situations are invented.

    Before you move on

    2. Fast beats perfect. The 24 hours in the policy is a limit, not a plan.
    3. Not sure? Report it. InfoSec would rather hear about a false alarm than miss a real one.
